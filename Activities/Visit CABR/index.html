Strengthening International Cooperation in Advanced Engineering Testing and Research

In March 2026, We were invited to visit the China Academy of Building Research (CABR) in Beijing, China, one of the country's leading institutions in building science, engineering research, and technical innovation.

The visit provided a valuable opportunity to exchange technical knowledge, explore potential areas of collaboration, and share technical expereince in construction materials testing, wind tunnel testing, and siesmic research.

The proposed collaboration focuses on strengthening technical cooperation and facilitating access to advanced testing facilities and engineering expertise in the following key areas:

CABR Stone Monument
CABR National Testing Center Building

Key Areas of Technical Collaboration

1. Construction Materials Testing

  • - Testing and evaluation of construction materials.
  • - Quality assurance and compliance assessment.
  • - Advanced laboratory testing and technical evaluation.

2. Wind Tunnel Testing

  • - Wind engineering studies for high-rise buildings and complex structures.
  • - Wind load assessment and structural response analysis.
  • - Pedestrian-level wind comfort and environmental studies.
  • - Technical support for optimizing building performance and structural design.

3. Seismic Testing and Research

  • - Seismic performance evaluation of structural systems.
  • - Earthquake simulation and structural response testing.
  • - Seismic assessment of building components and structural assemblies.
  • - Research and technical support for improving structural safety and resilience.

Building Bridges for Engineering Excellence

This engagement represents an important step in our commitment to expanding international technical networks and strengthening engineering capabilities in Cambodia.

Through potential collaboration with CABR, we aim to promote knowledge exchange, facilitate access to internationally recognized testing and research facilities, and support the delivery of reliable, innovative, and technically advanced engineering solutions for high-rise buildings and complex developments.

We remain committed to connecting international engineering expertise with Cambodia's growing construction and development industry.
